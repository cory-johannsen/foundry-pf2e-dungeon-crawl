## Statements of systematic failure
- Dark-palette subjects: "SDXL lightens the background to grey/white for subjects whose own prompt describes a predominantly dark/black palette"; confirmed on 5 creatures that all failed ComfyUI and succeeded first try on Gemini: black-dracolisk, nuckelavee, roc, vampire-mastermind, storm-lord (commit 3593c6d, comment 57). Related but not the same: wraith and greater-shadow rendered pale/white shapes, fixed by "solid black shadow blending into background" phrasing.
- Elemental/scenery-implying concepts (storm clouds, ocean waves, fire genies, fire dragons): ComfyUI hit rate "settled around 30-40%" at L9 vs "~90%+" manual Gemini (comment 54). Fire/aura/ambient-effect subjects fill the frame (L10 dragons, fire-giant).
- Hybrid creatures (#252 log): 5 of 6 flagged first pass; hybrid + owlbear routed Gemini-first (data/token-art-routing.json). Dragons 9/18, undead 5/9, fey 3/5 flagged; "watch" dragon-young 5/8, fey 3/5.
- Young/ancient dragon "(Young)" variants and head-only cropping (5 dragons in #252 came back head/neck only).
- Unusual anatomy ignored even by Gemini: urglid (mouth on chest), hyakume (hundreds of eyes -> two eyes), ulgrem-lurann.
- Standing rule: creatures defer to Gemini backend after 3 ComfyUI attempts (commit 53141d3); Gemini succeeded on first call for nearly all deferred (L9 batch 16/16), except Mobogo (opaque white, later clean).
- Gemini prompt lesson: SDXL-style comma-soup negative list buried constraints; short imperative system prompt fixed redos (commit 3ccfdc4).
